Approach

Understand first.
Then build.

  1. Understand the task

    What makes your work harder? What already works? We define the goal and the limits.

  2. Plan a small solution

    We review scope, priorities and the first useful result. We agree a price based on the task.

  3. Build and verify

    We build a working solution and review how to use it together.

  4. Hand over and explain

    You receive source code, access to your accounts, a short guide and training.

Your website and source code are handed over to accounts you own. You receive a record of the work and agreed next steps. Post-launch care is arranged separately.

What you receive

We go through the finished solution together, so you know where to find everything and how to use it.

  • Source code and access under your own accounts.
  • A short guide and training for everyday use.
  • A record of completed work and agreed next steps.

You can continue with another developer. Care after launch is a separate service, agreed with you in advance.

How we check the result

We agree what the solution needs to do before work starts. Before handover, we check the main tasks, common error cases and, for websites, how it works on mobile.

The handover record explains what is finished, what we checked and which next steps make sense. Any changes beyond the agreed scope are discussed first.
