Enquiries and communication

The form requires your name, email and message so I can reply. Category, budget and timing are optional; an empty category is sent as a general enquiry. I also process the language and page path, and where relevant the website you asked to check and the referring domain.

An enquiry is not an order or advertising consent. Please do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

Purposes and legal bases

I use your enquiry to reply and take steps towards a possible contract at your request (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). For a business representative, legitimate interest in business communication applies (Article 6(1)(f)).

Without contact details I cannot reply. Website security and abuse prevention are legitimate interests. Records required by accounting or other laws are kept under the relevant legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

Retention

I delete unsuccessful enquiries and related correspondence 6 months after the last substantive contact. If we work together, necessary contract and accounting records are kept for the cooperation and applicable statutory periods or the defence of legal claims.

Provider backups expire under their recovery policies and are not used for further marketing. I do not send a newsletter.

Hosting, security and email

Cloudflare provides the website and enquiry database; Cloudflare Turnstile protects the form. Cloudflare processes IP addresses and technical information. Abuse prevention stores pseudonymous counters in Cloudflare D1: 24-hour windows for enquiries and confirmation emails, and 10-minute windows for automated measurements. Keys are derived from IP or email using a one-way digest; this is not anonymous data. Expired counters are removed in bounded batches during subsequent requests. On an inactive site they may physically remain longer, but no longer limit measurements or sending; provider backups expire under their own rules.

Resend sends notifications and possible confirmation emails; Fastmail handles incoming domain mail. Receipt on this website does not guarantee delivery of a confirmation email.

Automated website check

Only when you request an automated check, the public website address you enter is sent to Google PageSpeed Insights. Do not enter private URLs or links containing access tokens. The result is an automated indication, not a manual professional audit.

Optional analytics and advertising

Google Analytics measures visits only after analytics consent, when configured. Seznam Sklik SEM measures page views and received enquiries and supports remarketing only after advertising consent. These providers may process IP addresses, cookie identifiers and technical information; this is not a promise of anonymous measurement.

I do not send your name, email or enquiry content, and remove URL query parameters and fragments before measurement. Consent is optional.

Changing your choice

Open Cookie settings in the footer to accept, reject or withdraw analytics and advertising separately. Optional providers are not loaded before your choice.

Your preference is stored in the browser for 180 days, then requested again. Withdrawal stops further measurement and removes accessible measurement cookies on this domain. Third-party cookies can also be removed in browser settings. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Providers and international transfers

Cloudflare, Resend, Fastmail and, when enabled, Google and Seznam may process data through their international infrastructure. Their policies and contractual safeguards, including standard contractual clauses where applicable, are described here: Cloudflare, Resend, Fastmail, Google, Seznam.

You may request details about your data and the safeguards used by email.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and, where applicable, portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent in Cookie settings.

Write to siroky@radeq.cz; I normally respond within one month. You can complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection. I do not use automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.
