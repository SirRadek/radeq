Service protocol & guarantee

Every project has a clear output and verified performance.

For every project, you receive a service protocol - what I did, what changed, and what comes next (including what is not worth solving). Before handoff, I test the website: forms, mobile, speed, SEO basics, and measurement. I watch fast loading and write the agreed qualitative criterion into the protocol upfront - if I do not meet it at handoff, I return 15% of the labour price or fine-tune it without extra charge.
